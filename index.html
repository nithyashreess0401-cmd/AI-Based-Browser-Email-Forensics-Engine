Forensic Engine Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Forensic Engine is a student project that helps users analyze browser-history and email evidence for digital-forensics purposes.

Information accessed

  • Browser analysis: If you choose to use the browser extension, it may read browser history within the permissions shown by your browser and process the records you choose to analyze.
  • Gmail analysis: If Gmail integration is enabled, the app will request Google authorization and access only the Gmail data allowed by the permissions shown on Google's consent screen. You can decline or revoke access.
  • Reports: Evidence and analysis results may be processed to produce a forensic report.

How information is used

Information is used to run the analysis you request and generate results or reports. Do not analyze information unless you have permission to access it.

Storage and retention

The project does not currently use a database to persist evidence. However, the app may create temporary files or reports during processing, and hosting or service logs may be handled by the services that run the app. Do not assume that evidence is instantly or permanently erased unless the app's implementation and hosting configuration have been verified. Avoid submitting sensitive evidence unless you understand these limitations.

Sharing and third-party services

Forensic Engine does not intentionally sell forensic evidence. The app is hosted by Streamlit and may use Google sign-in/Gmail services. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may be transmitted to these services as needed to operate the features you request.

Security

This is a student project and is not a substitute for an independently audited forensic or evidence-management system. Do not submit evidence you are not authorized to process. Use appropriate safeguards when handling sensitive data and downloaded reports.

Your choices

You can choose not to use browser or Gmail analysis. You can revoke Google account access through your Google Account's third-party access settings. Delete any reports downloaded to your own device when they are no longer needed.

Children's privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the project changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
